# Lao Tzu

Lao Tzu is a foundational figure in Chinese philosophy, traditionally credited with the *Dao De Jing* (*Tao Te Ching*).

His teachings center on the **Dao** (“the Way”), an underlying pattern of reality that can’t be fully defined—only approached through reflection and observation.

## Design Principle

A core idea is **wu wei**, often translated as “non-action”: act in a way that aligns with reality rather than forcing outcomes through excessive control.

He also favors restraint over ambition, and simplicity over unnecessary striving.

The aim is not rigid complexity, but balance and effective alignment with how things unfold.

## Example

```text
Pushing too hard creates friction.
Let responsiveness guide action.
Choose humility, clarity, and moderation.
```
